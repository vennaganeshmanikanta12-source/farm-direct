The AI Layer

What makes this more than a marketplace

Five AI-assisted modules run underneath the buying and selling experience — recommending prices, forecasting demand, and flagging produce quality so both sides make better decisions.

Module 1

💰 Price Prediction

Suggests a fair selling price using historical prices, demand, season, location, and current supply. The farmer always decides whether to accept it.

Tomato: farmer price ₹25/kg → AI range ₹27–₹30/kg
Module 2

📈 Demand Forecasting

Predicts which produce customers are likely to want next, using past orders, search activity, and seasonal trends.

Expected tomato demand next week: High
Module 3

📷 Quality Classification

An image-assisted classifier gives produce an indicative freshness label — Fresh, Average, or Poor — to support (not replace) the farmer's own judgement.

Prototype note: appearance-based, not a food-safety test
Module 4

🧠 Recommendation Engine

Recommends produce to customers based on previous purchases, searches, location, season, and price range.

Search: "organic vegetables" → Carrot, Tomato, Spinach, Cucumber
Module 5

💬 AI Farmer Assistant

A chat assistant farmers can ask things like "what price should I sell my tomatoes for?" or "what's in high demand this week?"

Try it live in the Farmer Dashboard
